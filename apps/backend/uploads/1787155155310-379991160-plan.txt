### Total

This tracker contains **23 sections** and roughly **300 individual checkpoints** when concepts, algorithms, patterns, and practice categories are counted.

But here's the important distinction:

**300 checkboxes ≠ 300 things you need to memorize.**

Your real objective is to get to the point where, when you see a new problem, you can say:

> "This looks like sliding window / binary search / prefix sum / DFS / heap / DP..."

**That pattern-recognition skill is what gets you through interviews.**
